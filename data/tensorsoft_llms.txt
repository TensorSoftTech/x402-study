# data-api

> Market intelligence API: perpetual-futures funding rates and open interest
> (30+ venues), lending/borrow rates (Aave, Kamino, Morpho, ...), staking APRs,
> DEX pools, tokenized real-world assets and tokenized stocks — with dossiers,
> screens and time-series history up to 30 days.

Auth & access:
- Free tier: no credentials. 1 req/s per IP, 2500 calls/calendar-month.
  History capped at 24h. Watch `x-tier` / `x-quota-remaining` headers.
- API key: `X-API-Key` header (or bearer via `POST /auth/token`). 20 req/s
  default per key, no monthly cap, history to 30 days.
- x402 payment (when enabled): per-call USDC/GUSD via `402 Payment Required`
  challenges; see `GET /.well-known/x402` for machine-readable pricing across
  Base, Polygon, Solana and Robinhood Chain. A paid call unlocks the full 30-day
  history: $0.001 base + $0.0005/day beyond day one (+$0.01 for complex
  endpoints). In practice each lane floors its price a healthy margin over its
  settlement fee: the Coinbase CDP lanes (Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, Solana)
  carry a flat fee and floor near $0.003–0.004; BSC via payai can reach ~$0.012
  for micro-calls; Robinhood Chain honors the $0.0044 GUSD protocol minimum;
  multi-day range calls clear every floor unchanged — the live quote in the 402
  challenge is authoritative. A paid call does not consume the free monthly
  quota.

Spec & docs:
- OpenAPI 3.1: https://api.tensorsoft.tech/api-docs/openapi.json
- Swagger UI: https://api.tensorsoft.tech/swagger-ui
- x402 catalog: https://api.tensorsoft.tech/.well-known/x402

Endpoints (all under `/api/v1`, JSON, ETag/304 supported):
- perp: funding/latest · funding/history · funding/spread · funding/platforms · carry
- lend: latest · history
- staking: products
- pools: latest · history (needs `chain=`)
- rwa: assets · assets/{external_id} · {underlying} · {underlying}/deviation ·
  screen · contracts · lookup · issuer-tokens · polymarket · perps
- stocks: ohlc/{symbol} · profile/{ticker} · quotes · tokenized/{symbol} · listings
- dashboard: overview
- meta: platforms · datasets

Units: APRs are percentages (0.01 = 0.01%); funding_rate_apr annualized;
utilization 0–1. Over-limits answer 429 (`Retry-After`), quota exhaustion 429
or 402-with-payment where x402 is enabled.
